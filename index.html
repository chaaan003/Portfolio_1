About

Quality is a discipline, not a checkpoint.

Christian's path into QA started with a simple conviction: software should work the way people expect it to, every time, everywhere. Over the past seven years, that conviction has shaped a career spent across enterprise websites, mobile applications, and large-scale localization programs for global brands.

Starting as a QA Analyst and growing into a QA Lead, Christian has built a reputation for catching what others miss — from rendering issues across a dozen browsers to accessibility gaps that block real users. Along the way, the role expanded from executing test cases to designing test strategy, mentoring analysts, and owning release sign-off for enterprise programs spanning multiple regions.

Today, Christian leads QA for enterprise web and mobile initiatives at WPP Production, partnering with distributed teams across time zones to ship software that's fast, accessible, and genuinely reliable.
